# Prompts for optional AI reference images (Google Flow or similar)

Use the same base description for every view so the design stays consistent, and compare every result with
`design-sheet.png` (proportions win over the generated image). Reject images with badges, logos, licence-plate
text or recognisable production cars.

**Base description (paste first in every prompt):**
> Original modern four-door executive saloon, no badges or logos, deep blue metallic paint, 4.8 m long with a long
> hood and short front overhang, cab-rearward glasshouse with a gently curved roof and raked rear window ending on a
> short trunk lid, crisp shoulder crease running from the headlamps to the tail lamps, sculpted lower doors with
> gloss-black sills, slim LED headlamps with three projector modules, wide trapezoidal grille with a fine diamond
> mesh and one horizontal chrome blade, vertical corner intakes, full-width slim LED tail lamps with three
> horizontal light bars, rectangular exhaust finishers in a gloss-black diffuser, 19-inch ten-spoke turbine alloy
> wheels, chrome window surround, studio photograph on a dark grey floor, soft overhead softbox lighting.

1. **front34.png** — "…, front three-quarter view from the driver's side at eye level, 35 mm lens, whole car in frame."
2. **interior.png** — "…, interior seen from the open driver's door: black leather sports seats with bolsters,
   wide dashboard with a horizontal aluminium inlay and four slim air vents, driver display under a small hood,
   floating central touchscreen, three-spoke steering wheel, centre console with a gear selector and rotary
   controller, no logos."
3. Optional consistency checks: "exact side view, orthographic look, 85 mm lens, camera at wheel-centre height",
   "exact front view", "exact rear view" — only to compare against the design sheet, never instead of it.
